Hey, Sakshi! 💗

First of all, I hope you're doing okay. And if you're not, that's okay too.

I don't know exactly how things have been feeling inside your head lately, but I wanted to make something for you. Something you can come back to whenever you need a little reminder that someone genuinely cares.

So, take a little breath, get comfortable, and read this without rushing. There's no hurry here. ♡

☁️ 🌸 ☁️

You don't have to be okay

Sakshi, I know sometimes overthinking makes even the smallest things feel incredibly heavy. Your mind can become a place where a thousand thoughts are happening at once.

But please remember, you don't have to pretend everything is fine just because people expect you to be happy.

You are allowed to have difficult days. You are allowed to feel confused, tired, emotional or upset. None of that makes you any less wonderful.

🦋 💗 🦋

Be gentle with yourself

Please don't be angry with yourself for having feelings you cannot easily explain. You aren't supposed to have everything figured out.

Take things one day at a time. Give yourself permission to rest. Celebrate the smallest improvements.

And when things feel overwhelming, remember that asking for help is a brave and sensible thing to do.

Keep following your doctor's advice and take care of yourself. You deserve support without feeling guilty about needing it.

🌼 🌷 🌼

About that cute little you...

I hope you never forget how lovely you are. Not just because of your smile or your cuteness, but because you're a whole person with your own thoughts, dreams and little quirks.

You don't lose your sparkle just because you're having a difficult time. Even flowers have days when they need a little more sunshine and care.

So please, don't measure your worth by how happy or productive you feel today. You're enough even on the quiet days.

🤍 ☁️ 🤍

A small promise from my side

I might not always know the perfect thing to say, and I definitely won't pretend I can solve every problem.

But I can listen. I can remind you to take a break. I can share a little nonsense when you need a distraction.

And I can respect your silence when you don't feel like talking.

You don't owe me a happy version of yourself. You can simply be yourself.

🌸 🧸 🌸

Some little reminders...

♡ One difficult day doesn't define your life.

♡ You don't need to solve tomorrow's problems today.

♡ Rest is not a waste of time.

♡ You deserve kindness, especially from yourself.

♡ It's okay to ask someone you trust for help.

♡ You are appreciated, even when you don't feel like yourself.

And please remember that you don't have to go through everything alone.

💗 🦋 🌷

One last thing, Sakshi...

I hope the coming days are a little kinder to you. I hope you find moments of peace between all the noise, reasons to smile without forcing it, and people who make you feel safe being yourself.

Until then, take your time. No pressure to feel better quickly. No pressure to explain everything. Just one little step at a time.

And whenever you forget how special you are, come back and read this again.

With lots of care,
Someone who's always rooting for you ♡
